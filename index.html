Research Papers

AI-Driven Dynamic Food Menu Pricing for Restaurants Based on Demand and Weather

This research paper, titled "AI-Driven Dynamic Food Menu Pricing for Restaurants Based on Demand and Weather", focuses on the application of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in the restaurant industry. The study proposes an intelligent pricing system that dynamically adjusts food menu prices by analyzing historical sales data, customer demand patterns, and real-time weather conditions. The methodology incorporates data preprocessing, LSTM-based demand forecasting, weather impact analysis, and reinforcement learning-based price optimization. Experimental results demonstrated improved demand forecasting accuracy, a 13.8% increase in revenue, and a reduction in unsold inventory compared to traditional static pricing methods. The paper was published in the International Journal of Progressive Research in Engineering Management and Science (IJPREMS), Vol. 06, Issue 01, January 2026.

Artificial Intelligence • Machine Learning • Dynamic Pricing • Demand Forecasting • Weather Analytics 2026
